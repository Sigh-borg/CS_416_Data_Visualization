COVID-19 data: The New York Times COVID-19 Data

Population estimates are from the U.S. Census Bureau Population Estimates Program. Variant-period context is based on reporting from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

The map shows the increase in cumulative reported cases during the seven days ending on the selected date, divided by the corresponding state population and multiplied by 100,000. Negative retrospective corrections are displayed as zero.

Reported values are affected by testing availability, reporting practices, retrospective corrections, and changes in public-health surveillance.
